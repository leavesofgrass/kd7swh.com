Amateur radio firmware for ESP32 development boards: it bridges
packet-radio connections (the KISS serial protocol), acts as a
digipeater — a relay that repeats digital packets onward — and carries a
toolbox of radio utilities, on hardware that costs less than a coffee
order. The project where this site's interests actually converge.

C++. Full write-up to come; the code and README live at
