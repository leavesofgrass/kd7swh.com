# Jon — KD7SWH

Amateur radio operator in Oregon. I spend my time where Linux,
microcontrollers, and radio overlap — and in the rooms next door:
waveform analysis and sound synthesis, statistical analysis, and the
broader reaches of electrical engineering and computer science. This
site is where that gets written down.

Two threads run through all of it: **accessibility** and **open
standards**. Tools should work for everyone, on anything, and the formats
that matter should be the ones anyone can implement. This site practices
that — zero JavaScript, system fonts, real contrast, plain-text editions
of everything that matters.

I write C, Node.js, Python, and Rust, mostly for small machines and small
tools. On the air you'll find me on the ARRG K7RPT system, including the
Monday night [Linux User Net](/lun/info/), whose
[weekly link list](/lun/) this site carries.
