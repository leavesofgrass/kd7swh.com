Key Exam Facts — E3

  • MUF = critical frequency × sec(incidence angle)
  • D layer absorbs; E and F layers refract (bend) the wave back to Earth
  • Higher SFI → higher MUF → more bands open
  • Higher K-index → more absorption & ionospheric disturbance → worse conditions
  • F2 layer is the primary long-distance reflector — present day and night
  • Max single-hop F2 distance: ~4,000 km
  • Sporadic E can open 6 m and 10 m unexpectedly, independent of SFI
  • Night opens low bands (160/80 m); closes high bands (above ~10 MHz)
